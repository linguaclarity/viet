## Word: rồi

**Meaning**: already, then, later, or used to indicate the completion of an action

**Grammar**:
- **Part of Speech**: Adverb

**Usage**:
1. **Indicating Completed Actions**: "Rồi" is used to indicate that an action has been completed.
2. **Sequencing Events**: "Rồi" can be used to sequence events, meaning "then" or "after that."
3. **Expressing Certainty**: "Rồi" can also express certainty or assurance, often at the end of a statement.
4. **Combining with "đã"**: When combined with "đã," it emphasizes that an action has already been completed.

### Examples and Explanation

1. **Indicating Completed Actions**
   - **Sentence**: Tôi ăn cơm rồi.
   - **English Translation**: I have already eaten.
   - **Explanation**: "Rồi" indicates that the action of eating is complete.

2. **Sequencing Events**
   - **Sentence**: Tôi học xong, rồi tôi đi chơi.
   - **English Translation**: I finished studying, then I went out.
   - **Explanation**: "Rồi" is used to indicate the sequence of events, showing that going out happened after finishing studying.

3. **Expressing Certainty**
   - **Sentence**: Đừng lo, tôi biết rồi.
   - **English Translation**: Don't worry, I know already.
   - **Explanation**: "Rồi" is used to assure the listener that the speaker is aware of the information.

4. **Combining with "đã"**
   - **Sentence**: Cô ấy đã về nhà rồi.
   - **English Translation**: She has already come home.
   - **Explanation**: The combination of "đã" and "rồi" emphasizes that the action of coming home has been completed.

### Sentence Structure

- **Affirmative Statements**: Subject + Verb + rồi
  - **Example**: Anh ấy đi rồi. (He has gone already.)
    - **Anh ấy** (he) + **đi** (go) + **rồi** (already)

- **Sequencing Events**: Verb + rồi + Subject + Verb
  - **Example**: Học xong rồi tôi đi ngủ. (After finishing studying, I go to sleep.)
    - **Học xong** (finish studying) + **rồi** (then) + **tôi** (I) + **đi ngủ** (go to sleep)

### Situational Usage

1. **Casual Conversation**:
   - When casually mentioning that something has been done.
   - **Example**: Tôi đọc sách rồi. (I have read the book already.)

2. **Formal Contexts**:
   - In formal settings, "rồi" remains the same but the subject and verb may change to reflect politeness.
   - **Example**: Ông ấy đã ký hợp đồng rồi. (He has signed the contract already.)

3. **Expressing Urgency or Reassurance**:
   - Often used to express that there is no need to worry because something has been done.
   - **Example**: Đừng lo, mọi việc xong rồi. (Don't worry, everything is done.)

### Combined Example

**Sentence**: Bạn đã làm bài tập rồi chưa?
- **Meaning**: Have you done your homework yet?
- **Breakdown**:
  - **Bạn**: you (subject)
  - **đã**: already (past action marker)
  - **làm bài tập**: do homework (verb phrase)
  - **rồi**: already (indicating completion)
  - **chưa**: yet (question marker at the end)

### Summary

- **Placement**: "Rồi" is placed after the verb to indicate that an action has been completed.
- **Function in Sequencing**: "Rồi" can be used to sequence events, indicating what happens next.
- **Function in Questions**: Combined with "đã" and "chưa," it forms questions about whether an action has been completed.
- **Versatility**: "Rồi" can be used in various contexts to express completion, sequence, or assurance.
