 In Vietnamese, you can say "Do you want to have dinner?" as:

**Bạn có muốn ăn tối không?**

## Breakdown:
- **Bạn**: you (subject)
- **có muốn**: do want (auxiliary verb + main verb)
  - **có**: do (used to form questions)
  - **muốn**: want
- **ăn tối**: have dinner (verb + noun)
  - **ăn**: eat
  - **tối**: evening (referring to dinner)
- **không**: question particle (used to turn the statement into a question)

So, the full sentence "Bạn có muốn ăn tối không?" literally translates to "Do you want to eat dinner?" in English.
